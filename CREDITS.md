## 3D Assets, Textures & Audio
- All 3D habitat modules, rockets, astronauts, planetary terrains, floor grids, and skyboxes are **original procedural Three.js geometry and HTML5 Canvas textures** created specifically for ORBITAL SURVIVAL.

- All sound effects are procedurally synthesized in-browser via the **Web Audio API**.
