# NASA Science & Educational Sources

- **NASA Space Apps Challenge — Build a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer**: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/junior-astronaut-mission-trainer/ — Supports the 4 engineering pillars (Life Support, Radiation Shielding, Power, Food).
- **NASA ECLSS Subsystems Overview**: https://www.nasa.gov/reference/jsc-life-support-subsystems/ — Supports separating Water Electrolysis Oxygen Generation from CO₂ Scrubbing.
- **NASA ISS Water Recovery Milestone**: https://www.nasa.gov/missions/station/iss-research/nasa-achieves-water-recovery-milestone-on-international-space-station/ — Supports the Water Recycler and closed-loop hydration gameplay.
- **NASA Apollo 13 & CO₂ Filter Incident**: https://www.nasa.gov/ochmo-tb-004-carbon-dioxide-2/ — Supports Scenario 3 and the CO₂ Filter/Adapter Puzzle (Plan B).
- **NASA Orion Crew Systems & Radiation Shelter**: https://www.nasa.gov/reference/crew-systems/ — Supports Scenario 4, the Radiation Shelter bay, and hydrogen-rich Water-Barrier Blocks.
- **NASA Moon Trek & Mars Trek**: https://trek.nasa.gov/moon/ and https://trek.nasa.gov/mars/ — Supports Lunar (`0.16g`) and Martian (`0.38g`, dust storm) environmental design.
